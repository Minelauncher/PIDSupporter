[h1]PIDSupporter - Tune Helper[/h1]
Mod latest version 1.1.5

An in-game helper for tuning PID controllers in From the Depths. Collect a response, review suggested settings, and apply them when you are ready.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]30-second response recordings with automatic preparation.
[*]PID suggestions with predicted performance and response information.
[*]Manual Apply and Restore controls.
[*]Combine up to five recordings from the same PID window.
[*]Response-band displays and an in-game guide.
[/list]

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Enable the mod, restart the game, and open the PID window's Auto Tune tab.
[*]Start collection under representative operating conditions. Preparation adjusts the working gain, then a 30-second recording begins. The setpoint moves during collection, so allow room for the vehicle to respond.
[*]Review the suggestion, select Apply, and try it in normal operation. Use Restore to return to the original settings.
[*]To add more data, collect again without closing the PID window. Closing it stops collection and clears its recording history.
[/list]

[h2]About[/h2]
The helper fits a model to the recorded response and searches for settings that track well without unnecessarily abrupt control. It also checks nearby PID settings and sensitivity to model error.

English and Korean theory notes are included for anyone interested in the details.
